Safety and evidence

Evidence Before Inference

  • Electron distinguishes USB detection, an active PM3 session and measured diagnostics.
  • Unknown or unsupported hardware state remains visible instead of being guessed.
  • Card scans are temporary observations until the user deliberately saves the information to their RFID Collection.
  • Remove keys, dumps and personal or payment-card data before sharing feedback or reports.
