About

A local-first RFID Collection and evidence platform

Electron helps acquire, understand and preserve RFID evidence. Its Collection remains the durable centre while scans, devices and analysis sessions provide temporary observations.

Philosophy

Evidence Before Inference

Electron separates observed facts from interpretation, keeps uncertainty visible and leaves meaningful acceptance or hardware decisions under user control.

Direction

Capabilities instead of forced setup

Hardware support is based on verified capabilities. Compatible stock firmware supports the core workflows, while additional Device Studio insight appears only when the connected firmware reports it.

Background intelligence

Electron Engine

The Electron Engine is the background intelligence layer that supplies structured connection, compatibility and diagnostic information to the interface. It is not a separate workspace, page or action.

Website privacy

Privacy-first visitor statistics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page views, visits and website performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not track individual visitors or collect or use visitors' personal data.
